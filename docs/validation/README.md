# Phase 8 — consent-based validation kit

Prepared 31 October 2026. **No recruitment, sessions or return use have occurred.** The target is eight independent designers, builders or writers who already reuse prompts/files in AI work. This kit is ready for review; use the signed, accepted candidate before inviting people to install. Outreach needs an explicit instruction naming the recipients/channel. This task does not send invitations.

## Files and privacy

- [Protocol and consent](protocol.md): baseline, neutral tasks, assistance rules and follow-up.
- [Recruitment draft](recruitment.md): ready to review before authorized outreach.
- [Observation template](observation-template.csv): one row per task and participant.
- [Return-use template](return-use-template.csv): manual diary, one row per day.
- [Report template](report-template.md): proposed thresholds and honest decision record.

Copy templates to ignored `local-data/phase8/` before filling them. Keep consent/contact details separately from participant IDs; don't commit names, email addresses, working prompts, libraries, recordings or raw session notes. Publish only an anonymized aggregate report after review. The blank templates are structure, not participant evidence.

Suggested retention for review: delete contacts and raw observations 30 days after the final report, honor earlier withdrawal, and retain only consented anonymous aggregates. The owner must confirm the actual contact, withdrawal route and retention before consent is collected.

## Decisions to evaluate

The PRD proposes six of eight completing the core flow unaided, five of eight using Nivlet on at least three separate days within seven days, and four of eight choosing to keep it installed after the week. Nivlet is free. Purchase intent, checkout and licence recovery are retired checks, not missing commercial evidence. These are product decision thresholds with a small, convenience sample; they are not proof of broad demand.

Record failures, assistance, missing diaries, dropouts and withdrawals separately. Never silently shrink eight to a smaller denominator or count multiple sessions on one date as multiple days. Missing follow-up is unknown, not evidence of return use or keeping the app. If the final eligible cohort is not eight, explain it and rerun or revise the study rather than reporting the original gate as passed.
