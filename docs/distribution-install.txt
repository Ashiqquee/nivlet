Nivlet — install, update and recover

Nivlet is free. No account, licence or payment is needed.
It needs a Mac with Apple silicon.

Install
1. Drag Nivlet onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Eject the "Nivlet" disk image (drag it to the Trash or press Eject in Finder).
3. Open Nivlet from your Applications folder, not from this disk image.
4. Nivlet lives in the menu bar and in a tab at the edge of your screen.
   It has no Dock icon. Open it again from Spotlight to show the panel.

If macOS says it could not verify Nivlet
Early versions are not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops the first launch.
1. Click Done. Do not click Move to Trash.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
3. Next to the message about Nivlet, click Open Anyway, then confirm with
   your password or Touch ID. You only need to do this once per version.
On macOS 14, you can instead Control-click Nivlet in Applications and choose
Open. You never need to turn off Gatekeeper.

Update to a new version
1. In Nivlet, export all pockets and keep the export somewhere private.
2. Fix any visible save error, then quit Nivlet from its menu-bar menu.
3. Open the new disk image and drag Nivlet onto Applications, choosing Replace.
4. Eject the disk image, open Nivlet and check your prompts, references and notes.
Replacing the app keeps your library. You never need to delete it to update.

If something goes wrong
- If a new version won't start, keep your library as it is and reinstall the
  previous version you trust. Never delete the library to make the app start.
- An older version may refuse a library that a newer version has updated.
  That protects your data. Restore a full backup made before the update,
  with Nivlet closed, and keep the newer library separately.
- Restoring an export adds its pockets as new pockets after showing a preview.
  It never silently replaces what you already have.

Uninstall
Quit Nivlet from its menu-bar menu, then move Nivlet from Applications to the
Trash. Your library stays in ~/Library/Containers/ashique.nivlet.Nivlet until
you delete that folder too. Export anything you want to keep first.

on the clipboard; you choose what to paste or attach in other apps.
