## Standalone mark

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: standalone logomark master for Nivlet, a native Mac utility that keeps reusable prompts, reference files and scratch notes in project pockets in a compact screen-edge panel.
Primary request: Design one original, exceptionally refined geometric N monogram, suitable for an ambitious YC-quality software brand. The concept is "the folded shelf": two bold folded tab/ribbon forms interlock to imply an uppercase N and the act of tucking useful things into a pocket. A clear N silhouette at first glance; subtle shelf/pocket association second.
Style/medium: precise flat vector-like graphic, optically balanced, restrained modern identity. Broad solid strokes, softly eased outer corners, sharp intentional interior negative spaces, simple enough to identify at 16 pixels. Aim for custom recognizable geometry rather than an ordinary typed N. Quiet, clever, useful.
Composition: SINGLE centered isolated symbol on a square transparent canvas, occupying about 72% of width and height. Front-facing orthographic. No wordmark and no presentation board.
Color: one solid near-black ink #17191C, no other colors. Actual transparent background with clean alpha edges.
Constraints: brand-new geometry. References are modern startup identity principles of simplicity, strong silhouette and separate mark/wordmark, not the shape of any existing logo. Avoid copying Raycast, Linear, Notion, Resend or Pinterest marks. No thin lines, no repeated speed stripes, no sparkle, no chatbot bubble, no brain, no mountain, no generic folder outline, no 3D, no bevel, no gradients, no texture, no cast shadow, no lettering beyond the monogram, no captions, no mockup.
